# Executive summary

## Selected opportunity

**Overtake** — a subscription web app that helps a Fantasy Premier League
manager beat *the specific people in their mini-league*, rather than beat the
global average.

Every existing paid FPL tool optimises one number: expected points. That is the
right objective for the ~0.001% chasing a top-10k global finish. It is the wrong
objective for almost everyone else, because almost everyone's actual goal is
finishing above eight friends, a group chat, or an office league. Those are
different problems with different answers. If you are 40 points behind the leader
with six gameweeks left, the highest-expected-points transfer is usually the
*wrong* move — you need variance, and specifically variance the leader is not
exposed to. No incumbent computes that, because none of them model your rivals.

The data to model them is free, public and real-time. The official FPL API
exposes, without authentication, every manager's squad for every gameweek, their
transfers, their chip usage, and full mini-league standings. **[verified]**

## Target audience

Primary: **18–30, male-skewing, English-speaking, in a competitive mini-league
of 5–30 people, playing FPL for the fourth season or more.** Secondary: 15–17
in school leagues (with the age handling in `11-legal-security-risk.md`), and
31–35 in workplace leagues. Geography: UK first, then Ireland / Nordics /
Netherlands / India-diaspora English-language markets. FPL had **13.10 million
registered managers in 2025/26** **[verified]**.

## Core human motivation

Status inside a small, named, permanent peer group. FPL's real currency is not
points — it is the group chat on Monday morning. The product's job is to make
you the person who wins that argument.

## Primary value mechanism

**Status**, supported by **Better decisions**, **Anxiety reduction** ("did I
just make a mistake before the deadline?"), **Curiosity** ("what is he actually
planning?") and **Reason to return** (a hard weekly deadline, ~38 times a season).

## Why people care

Existing free mini-league tools show *statistics*. Existing paid tools show
*projections*. Nobody answers the only question that matters to a mini-league
player: **"What do I have to do to overtake him?"**

## Why people pay

Because the paid FPL market already exists and is priced far above this product.
Fantasy Football Scout charges £10/month or £50/year; Fantasy Football Hub sells
tiered memberships with a "win your mini-league or your money back" guarantee —
which is direct evidence that the mini-league, not global rank, is the emotional
purchase driver. **[verified]** Overtake sells the thing that guarantee is about,
at €4.99/month or €29.99 for a full season pass.

## Why people return

The Premier League schedules the retention for you. There are ~38 deadlines a
season, each one a hard commitment device, plus daily price changes and injury
news between them. Your rivals' moves change your optimal move every single week.
This is a product whose core content is regenerated by the outside world 38 times
a year without the founder writing anything.

## AI advantage

AI is not the generator here; it is the interpreter on top of a simulation.

1. A **Monte Carlo engine** simulates the rest of the season 20,000 times over
   your squad and each rival's squad, producing P(finish above rival X).
2. An **LLM layer** turns that distribution into an argument a human acts on —
   naming the rival, the specific differential, the gameweek the gap closes, and
   what it costs if it goes wrong.
3. A **persistent league memory** accumulates every rival's behavioural history
   (do they take hits? do they chase last week's points? when do they wildcard?)
   and feeds it back as priors.

A general chatbot cannot do this. It does not know your squad, your rivals'
squads, current prices, or this week's injury news, and it cannot run 20,000
simulations. This passes the AI-replacement test on data access *and* on
computation, not on prompt quality.

## Subscription model

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | €0 | 1 league, live standings, squad diagnosis, **1 rival dossier per season** |
| Pro monthly | **€4.99/mo** | Unlimited rivals + leagues, Deadline Brief, Overtake Simulator, chip planner, Ask-the-Gaffer |
| Pro season pass | **€29.99/season** (Aug–May) | Same, one payment, no renewal decision mid-season |

Blended net ARPU €3.56/paid user/month (after verified Stripe UK/EEA card, tax and
billing fees); AI cost bottom-up **$0.10–0.59 per paid user per month**; gross
margin 66–87% depending on scale **[estimate]**. Break-even against a €25/month
infrastructure tier is **8 paying users**.

## The one-line verdict

Build it. It is a two-week MVP on free public data, in a market that has already
proven it pays subscriptions, with a mathematically distinct objective function
no incumbent optimises and a viral loop (mini-leagues *are* friend groups) that
costs nothing. Its three real risks — seasonality, the FPL API's undocumented
status, and incumbent bundling — are all named, quantified and mitigated in
`11-legal-security-risk.md`.

**What must be proven first:** that a mini-league player will pay for
rival-relative advice at all. That is testable in 14 days with the free tier and
one paywalled artefact (`12-mvp-14-day-plan.md`, day 13 GO/NO-GO).
